# WEGOTCHU: Team Meetings & Communication Cadence

## 1. Meeting Rhythm & Rituals

To maintain velocity across research and development:
* **Weekly Sprint Sync (Mondays, 10:00 AM):** 45-minute sprint planning, review completed issues, assign new issues.
* **Mid-Week Standup (Wednesdays, 15 minutes, Async/Sync):** Progress check, unblock dependencies.
* **Code & Research Review (Fridays, 4:00 PM):** Demonstrate running prototypes, review open PRs, evaluate experiment metrics.

---

## 2. Kickoff Meeting Agenda (Week 1)

* **Objective:** Align all 4 members on repository structure, architecture documents, and Week 1 deliverables.
* **Agenda Items:**
  1. Walkthrough of `README.md`, `system-architecture.md`, and `ai-architecture.md` led by Project Lead.
  2. Review of Data Contracts v0.1 in `data-flow.md`.
  3. Git workflow walkthrough (`CONTRIBUTING.md` and Conventional Commits).
  4. Review and assignment of Week 1 Foundation issues (#1 to #7) and initial domain issues.
  5. Setup of local development environments and hardware verification.
